# Pages and flows

- / — hero, category navigation, selected fragrances, collections, product sections, service information and footer.
- /collections and /collections/[slug] — searchable, filterable, sortable catalog, includes category/collection views.
- /products/[slug] — images, variant selector, price, quantity, notes, details, related products; Buy on WhatsApp includes name, size, quantity and product link.
- /search — full results, linked from header autocomplete.
- /wishlist — browser-local saved fragrance list.
- /bundle — configurable fragrance/size selection with WhatsApp message.
- /about, /contact, /faq, /policies/[slug] — editable plain-language information.
- /admin/login — owner sign-in.
- /admin — actual catalog counts and generation status.
- /admin/products — create/edit/archive/duplicate, variant pricing, stock, gallery, CSV export, bulk price rules.
- /admin/templates — master upload, per-size drag editor, text fitting, image proof and save.
- /admin/bulk — manual/CSV/XLSX parsing, validation, selected-size preview approval, resumable generation.
- /admin/content — categories, collections, navigation, banners, pages, homepage sections and settings.
